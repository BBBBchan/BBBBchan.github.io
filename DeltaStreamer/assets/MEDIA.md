# Media notes

The demo videos and project figures accompany DeltaStreamer. Example footage is from OVO-Bench and StreamingBench; underlying video rights remain with their original owners.

The instrumental soundtrack, **A Gentle Stream**, was composed and synthesized for this demo without third-party music or samples. It is included in the demo videos with a fade-in and fade-out. The project media and original soundtrack are shared under CC BY-NC 4.0; this does not grant additional rights to underlying benchmark footage.
